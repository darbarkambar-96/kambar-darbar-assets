What is deleted

Your Kambar Darbar app account, its available sign-in profile information, and the personal information in feedback associated with that account. Deletion runs after you confirm your identity; no staff approval is required.

The page reports success only after the app-account deletion has been acknowledged. If your connection fails, follow the on-screen instructions and try again. Some feedback may already have been removed during an interrupted attempt.

What stays separate

Your Google account and public devotional content, schedules and publications remain separate from this app account.

Downloads, notes, bookmarks, reading settings and other data stored only on your phone cannot be erased by this website. Use the app's reading-data reset or Android's app-storage controls to remove local data; files you saved outside the app may need to be removed separately.

Delete from the Android app instead

In a version with automatic deletion, sign in and open Settings → Privacy & account → Delete account and data. If your installed version offers only an email draft, use the self-service form above or update the app.

This page keeps its sign-in session only in memory. It does not store your Google password or keep a signed-in session after you close it.
