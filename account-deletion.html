Kambar Darbar: account and data deletion

Android app: org.kambardarbar.app. Published by BITSY INFOTECH.

You can request deletion without installing or signing in to the app.

Email darbarkambar@gmail.com with the subject Kambar Darbar app account and data deletion request. Send from the email address used in the app, or identify that address in your message so the Darbar team can verify which account and feedback belong to you.

Open an email request

Review the draft and send it yourself. Opening a draft does not submit a request.

What the request covers

Request removal of your Kambar Darbar app account, its sign-in profile, and associated personal information in submitted feedback. This does not delete your Google account.

Completion time and retention

The Darbar team may contact you to verify ownership. Within 30 days after ownership is verified, the team will delete the app's Firebase Authentication account and associated feedback and personal information from the active app database. These app records are not intentionally retained after the request is completed. The team will confirm completion by email.

Downloads, notes and reading settings stored only on your phone are separate from the online account. The app's Settings includes a reading-data reset; your phone's app-storage controls can remove the remaining local app data.

Public devotional content, event schedules and publications are separate from your sign-in account. Do not send passwords or identity documents with a request.
